Read before you cite this
  • Edge speeds are assumed from OSM maxspeed tags where present, road-class defaults where not — travel times are estimates, not measured.
  • Road geometry is from OpenStreetMap, clipped to a district extract — coverage gaps at the clip boundary are expected (see the false-positive case).
  • Health facilities (34) were hand-curated from OpenStreetMap (snapshot 16 Sep 2026); the selection rule and its remaining manual judgment calls are documented in scripts/facility_curation.py. Not an official or complete list. One facility (WIMS Urban Health Center, Kalpetta) is tagged private in OSM; its public/private status is unverified.
  • Village–facility connector lines on this map are straight-line indicators, not the routed path.
  • Verified through multiple checks: buffered re-extraction (to rule out boundary-clipping artifacts), a pre-event (2024) vs current network comparison, and testing under three different definitions of which roads count as usable routes. One nearby bridge’s status as a single point of failure remains ambiguous and depends on whether an unpaved track counts as a usable road — this is a documented limitation, not a resolved finding.
  • The bridge that failed in July 2024 (Chooralmala–Mundakkai) lies 1.1–3.3 km from the single-link cuts this analysis identifies; the analysis does not pinpoint that bridge.
