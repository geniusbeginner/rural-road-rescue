# 1e. Back-test candidates (collected only; nothing run)

Checked 2026-09-28. Excerpts are verbatim from raw page or PDF text. A source marked "secondary" is Wikipedia or a blog; "search snippet only" means I didn't confirm the text on the page.

**Summary of what each source records:**
- **2024 Mundakkai–Chooralmala (Wayanad):** isolated places, a bridge coordinate, and "only route" statements.
- **2019 Puthumala (Wayanad, same valley):** only "the area has been cut off".
- **2021 Koottickal/Kokkayar (Kottayam/Idukki):** a KSDMA table of 23+ landslide locations, and "Around 50 people were stranded in the Koottikkal village".
- **2020 Pettimudi (Idukki):** GSI records "Road and transmission lines damaged". The only bridge claim is on Wikipedia.
- **2019 Kavalappara (Malappuram):** "a walkover bridge [was] completely washed away".
- **2018 floods:** "Munnar, Wayanad isolated", via a Wikipedia citation only.

## 1. Mundakkai–Chooralmala, Wayanad, 30 July 2024 (inside our study district)
- **Reported isolated:** Mundakkai; also Attamala and Noolpuzha (connectivity disrupted).
- **Excerpts:**
  - GSI FIR (https://bhusanket.gsi.gov.in/Public_Portal_News_pdf/FIR_Mundakkai-Chooralmala.cleaned.pdf): "Collapse of bridge over Punapuzha, which is the only connective way of Mund akkai to Chooramala and other parts of Wayanad, isolated Mundakkai".
  - Scientific Reports 2025 (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-07828-3): "washed away the bridge near Chooralmala (11.4992° N, 76.1601° E), severing the critical connection between Chooralmala and Mundakkai"; "indirectly affected the villages of Attamala and Noolpuzha, disrupting their connectivity with the rest of the district".
- **Full detail:** see `landslide_location_check.md`.

## 2. Puthumala, Meppadi, Wayanad, 8 August 2019 (inside our study district)
- **Reported isolated:** the Puthumala area.
- **Excerpt (The News Minute, https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/when-wayanads-puthumala-faced-landslides-neighbours-came-rescue-106913):** "NDRF personnel have rescued 54 people from Puthumala in Meppadi. … The area has been cut off as there is no connectivity."
- **Supporting mention (GSI FIR 2024, above):** "On 9th August 2019, Puthumala, a massive and fatal debris flow occurred". The date differs (9 vs 8 August) between sources.
- **Search snippet only (not verified):** "road blocked at Kalladi area on the same day" and "3000 peoples evacuated from the region including Mundakkai and Chooralmala area". Its likely source is a ResearchGate paper, "Landslide events of Kerala, their causes and impacts: A Case study of Puthumala Landslide in Wayanad District", which I didn't open.
- **Gap:** no source I found names the specific road or bridge that failed.

## 3. Koottickal (Kottayam) and Kokkayar (Idukki), 16 October 2021
- **Reported isolated/stranded:** Koottickal village; locations in Kanjirappally and Meenachil taluks.
- **Excerpts (KSDMA event report, https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Event-report_October-2021.pdf):**
  - "Debris flow incidences were reported at the Elangad area in Koottikkal village (Kottayam district), Theekkekara village (Kottaya m), and a landslide incidence was reported at Edakunnam village. Around 50 people were stranded in the Koottikkal village."
  - "The major landslide occurred at Mackochi of Kokkayar village of Peerumedu taluk at about 11 am."
  - The report has a table, "REPORTED LANDSLIDE EVENTS IN KERALA BETWEEN 11-10-2021 to 25-10-2021", listing district, taluk, village, location and date. Examples: "13 Kottayam Kanjirappally Koottickal Kodunga 16-10-2021", "21 Kottayam Kanjirappally Koottickal Elamkadu 16-10-2021".
- **Gap:** the table lists landslide locations, not road cuts. No coordinates were extracted.

## 4. Pettimudi (Rajamala), Munnar, Idukki, 6 August 2020
- **Reported isolated:** Pettimudi.
- **Excerpts (GSI incident report, https://bhusanket.gsi.gov.in/Output/LS_Incidence_Report/2020/Landslide%20at%20Pettimudi,%20near%20Munnar,%20Idukki%20District,%20Kerala%20(06th%20August,%202020).pdf):**
  - "6 NH/SH/Locality : Eravikulam-KDH-Idamalakudi road", "7 Latitude : 10.171695°N", "8 Longitude : 77.011305°E", "33 Communication : Road and transmission lines damaged."
  - "Idamalakudi road also got flooded with debris."
- **Secondary (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Pettimudi_landslide):** "The bridge on the road was washed away because of which the rescuers could not reach in time." I didn't trace this to a primary source.
- **Secondary (AGU Landslide Blog, https://blogs.agu.org/landslideblog/2020/08/14/pettimudi-landslide/):** "Drone footage taken today shows a clear landslide close by the bridge … Obviously not confirmed this is what it hit, but given the bridge parapet has been wiped out it would fit." The blog author flags this as unconfirmed.
- **Search snippet only:** "the only one road connecting Pettimudi to the world outside was damaged". Not verified on a page.

## 5. Kavalappara, Pothukal, Malappuram, 8 August 2019
- **Reported:** communication cut off; bridge damage.
- **Excerpt (International Consortium on Landslides, https://www.landslides.org/report/kavalappara-landslide/):** "over 39 houses and a walkover bridge were completely washed away". Also: "69 casualties, 39 completely damaged houses along with roads, utilities and other infrastructure damages were recorded during our survey."
- **Search snippet only:** "At 12 noon, the flood level reached more than 2 m above the bridge at Bhoodanam in the Puthukkad area." Also: "communication was totally cut off". Not verified on a page.
- **Note:** a "walkover bridge" is a footbridge. It wouldn't be in our vehicle-only road graph.

## 6. Kerala floods, August 2018 (statewide)
- **Reported isolated:** "Munnar, Wayanad".
- **Secondary only (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Kerala_floods):** "caused severe landslides and left the hilly districts isolated." It cites a news headline: "Landslides hit several places in Malabar; Munnar, Wayanad isolated". I didn't open the cited article.
- **Gap:** these are district-scale "isolated" claims, with no specific links named. As a link-level back-test, that's weak.

## Inferences (mine)
- **Candidates 1 and 2** are inside our existing Wayanad graph, in the same Meppadi valley. They could be back-tested without new road data. Only candidate 1 has a coordinate for the failed link.
- **Candidates 3 and 4** would need new districts (Kottayam, Idukki).
- **Candidates 5 (footbridge) and 6 (district scale)** are poor matches for a single vehicle-road cut.
