## Result
- **Date:** 7 July 2026 (Tuesday), morning. Confirmed by 7 outlets independent of DownToEarth.
- **Location:** the exit/entrance area of the Anakkampoyil–Kalladi–Meppadi twin-tunnel construction site, "near Meenakshi Bridge at Kalladi".

  - **No primary government document found.** There's no Kalladi report on KSDMA's Events 2026 page, and no PIB or Kerala PRD page turned up.
